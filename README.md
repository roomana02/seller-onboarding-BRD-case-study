# Seller Onboarding & Requirements Case Study — E-commerce Marketplace

## Overview
A Business Analyst case study addressing a common e-commerce marketplace 
problem: new sellers taking too long to go live, leading to high drop-off 
during onboarding. This project simulates a full BA workflow from requirement 
gathering through to a proposed process redesign.

## What's Included
- **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** — background, scope, and business requirements
- **Functional Requirements (FRD-level detail)** — system behavior, inputs/outputs, and business rules for each requirement
- **User Stories & Acceptance Criteria** — Agile-format stories with MoSCoW prioritization and a sample sprint plan
- **Stakeholder Analysis** — Power/Interest mapping and a RACI matrix
- **AS-IS and TO-BE Process Flows** — visual diagrams showing current bottlenecks and the proposed improved process

## Skills Demonstrated
Requirement gathering, BRD/FRD documentation, stakeholder management, 
Agile user story writing, process mapping, and business analysis.
